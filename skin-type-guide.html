Skin Type Guide

Understanding your skin type is the first step to building a routine that works. Explore each skin type, its characteristics, and what we suggest for it.

Know Your Skin

Understanding Skin Types

Every skin type is unique. Select yours below to explore its characteristics, key ingredients, and a routine built around it.

Dry Skin Profile

Dry skin produces less sebum than other skin types. It may feel tight, rough or flaky — particularly after cleansing or in cold weather.

Common Characteristics

  • Skin feels tight, especially after cleansing
  • Visible flaking or rough texture in some areas
  • Rarely shiny or greasy throughout the day
  • Fine lines may appear more prominent due to dehydration
  • May feel uncomfortable in cold or dry climates

Suggested Active Ingredients

Hyaluronic Acid Ceramides Squalane Glycerin Shea Butter
Shop Products for Dry Skin

Oily Skin Profile

Oily skin produces more sebum than average. It may appear shiny across most of the face and may be more prone to congested or enlarged pores.

Common Characteristics

  • Shiny or greasy appearance across much of the face
  • Pores may appear larger, particularly on the nose and cheeks
  • Makeup may not wear well without a primer
  • More prone to blackheads, whiteheads or blemishes
  • Skin may feel greasy relatively quickly after cleansing

Suggested Active Ingredients

Niacinamide Salicylic Acid (BHA) Zinc Lactic Acid Green Tea Extract
Shop Products for Oily Skin

Combination Skin Profile

Combination skin shows oily characteristics in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) while cheeks and other areas may be normal or dry.

Common Characteristics

  • Oily forehead, nose and chin — drier cheeks
  • Enlarged pores in the T-zone; normal or tight elsewhere
  • May experience both breakouts and dry patches
  • Products designed for one extreme may exacerbate the other

Suggested Active Ingredients

Niacinamide Hyaluronic Acid Glycerin Light AHAs
Shop Products for Combination Skin

Normal Skin Profile

Normal skin is characterised by a balanced sebum level, minimal sensitivity, and few visible skin concerns. A maintenance-focused routine is most beneficial.

Common Characteristics

  • Balanced — not too oily or dry across most areas
  • Pores are small and not particularly visible
  • Tolerates most ingredients well
  • Breakouts are infrequent and minor
  • Focus is on maintaining equilibrium and prevention

Suggested Active Ingredients

Vitamin C Hyaluronic Acid Niacinamide Peptides
Shop Products for Normal Skin

Sensitive Skin Profile

Sensitive skin reacts more easily to products, ingredients and environmental triggers. A minimal, fragrance-free routine is generally most suitable.

Common Characteristics

  • Redness, stinging or itching after applying products
  • Reacts to fragrances, alcohols or preservatives
  • May experience flares with seasonal or climate changes
  • Products with fewer ingredients are typically better tolerated
  • Patch testing new products is strongly recommended

Suggested Active Ingredients

Centella Asiatica Ceramides Beta-Glucan Panthenol Allantoin
