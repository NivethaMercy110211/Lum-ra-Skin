4 Simple Steps for Healthy, Glowing Skin

Designed to cleanse, balance, treat and protect twice daily.

Cleanse

02

03
Treat
Apply targeted serums or treatments for your concern, such as hydration, texture or tone.
04
Protect & Seal
Finish with a moisturiser and SPF (AM) or a nourishing cream (PM) to lock everything in.

Know What's In Your Skincare

Ingredient Transparency

Every Luméra formula begins with evidence-informed ingredient selection. Here are six hero ingredients that support the core of our range.

Hyaluronic Acid
A humectant molecule that may help draw and retain moisture within the skin. Found in our hydration serums and moisturisers.
Hydration
Niacinamide
A form of Vitamin B3 that may support skin tone, pore appearance and sebum balance. Well-tolerated across most skin types.
Clarity
Ceramides
Lipids that are naturally found in the skin's barrier. Ceramide formulas may help support moisture retention and barrier resilience.
Barrier
Vitamin C
L-Ascorbic Acid is a studied antioxidant ingredient that may support a more radiant and even skin tone over time.
Radiance
Peptides
Short amino acid chains designed to communicate with the skin. Found in our eye care and barrier support formulations.
Firmness
Squalane
A lightweight, plant-derived emollient that may support skin softness and moisture barrier function without clogging pores.
Nourishing

Understanding Your Skin

Find Your Skin Type

Your skin type shapes which products, ingredients and routines are most likely to support your skin's needs. Learn about each type below.

Dry Skin
May feel tight or flaky; benefits from rich hydrating formulas
Oily Skin
Produces excess sebum; suited to lightweight, balancing products
Combination
Oily T-zone with drier cheeks — needs a balanced approach
Normal Skin
Well-balanced skin; focus on maintenance and prevention
Sensitive Skin
Reacts easily; requires fragrance-free, minimal formulations
