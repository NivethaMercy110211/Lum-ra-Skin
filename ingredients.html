Ingredient Transparency

Every ingredient, clearly explained. We believe you deserve to know exactly what goes into your skincare — and why.

Our Approach

Our Ingredient Philosophy

Every ingredient in every Luméra formula is selected based on peer-reviewed evidence, safety profile and skin compatibility. We do not use ingredients for marketing appeal — only for demonstrable function.

Our formulations disclose all INCI names, concentrations where meaningful, and the function each ingredient serves. This page is a living document — updated whenever our formulations evolve.

Note: Luméra products are cosmetic formulations. Ingredient descriptions reflect cosmetic function — not medical or therapeutic claims. Individual results will vary.

Core Actives

Hero Ingredients

The foundation ingredients that appear across the core Luméra range.

Hyaluronic Acid
Sodium Hyaluronate
A humectant molecule that may help draw and retain water within the skin. Luméra uses a multi-molecular-weight blend to support surface and deeper hydration. Well-tolerated across all skin types, including sensitive skin.
HydrationAll Skin TypesAM + PM
Niacinamide
Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)
A water-soluble form of Vitamin B3 studied to support skin tone evenness, sebum balance and pore appearance. A well-researched cosmetic active, suited to oily, combination and acne-prone skin types.
ClarityOil ControlAM + PM
Ceramides
Ceramide NP, Ceramide AP, Ceramide EOP
Lipid molecules naturally present in the skin's outer layer. Ceramide-based formulas may support barrier function and moisture retention, particularly for dry and sensitive skin experiencing barrier disruption.
BarrierDry SkinSensitive
Vitamin C
L-Ascorbic Acid
L-Ascorbic Acid is a widely studied form of Vitamin C. At 15%, it may support antioxidant protection and a radiant, even skin appearance. Best used in morning routines; store away from direct sunlight.
AntioxidantRadianceAM Use
Peptides
Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7
Short amino acid chains that support signals linked to skin firmness and resilience. Used in eye care and targeted treatment formulas, Matrixyl 3000 is a studied peptide complex suited to morning and evening use.
FirmnessEye CareAM + PM
Squalane
Squalane (sugarcane-derived)
A lightweight plant-derived emollient similar to the skin's natural squalene. It may support barrier function and softness without feeling greasy. Non-comedogenic and well-tolerated by most skin types, including sensitive.
EmollientAll Skin TypesAM + PM
Moisture Science

Hydration Ingredients

These ingredients support the skin's ability to attract, bind and retain water.

Glycerin
Glycerol
One of the most effective and well-tolerated humectants in skincare. Glycerin draws moisture from the environment and may help maintain skin hydration throughout the day.
HumectantAll Skin Types
Panthenol
D-Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5)
A provitamin that may help support moisture retention and skin softness. Often included in formulas designed for post-cleanse recovery, sensitive skin repair and daily barrier care.
HydrationSoothing
Beta-Glucan
Beta-1,3/1,6-Glucan
Derived from oat or yeast, beta-glucan is a polysaccharide humectant that may help soothe and hydrate the skin. Often used in formulas for sensitive and reactive skin types.
SoothingSensitive Skin
Barrier Integrity

Barrier-Support Ingredients

These ingredients may help reinforce the skin's outermost protective layer.

Cholesterol
Cholesterol (plant-derived)
A key lipid found naturally in the skin barrier. When combined with ceramides and fatty acids, it may support the structural integrity of the skin's outer layer and reduce transepidermal water loss.
BarrierDry Skin
Fatty Acids
Linoleic Acid, Oleic Acid
Essential fatty acids that may help maintain the integrity of the skin barrier and support its lipid matrix. Found naturally in many plant-derived oils used in our formulations.
BarrierNourishing
Shea Butter
Butyrospermum Parkii Butter
A rich natural emollient from the shea tree that may help support skin softness and moisture. Used in richer, PM formulations for dry and sensitive skin types and compromised barriers.
EmollientDry Skin
Environmental Defence

Antioxidant Ingredients

These ingredients may help defend the skin against oxidative stress from environmental exposure.

Vitamin E
Tocopherol
A fat-soluble antioxidant that may help protect the skin from free radical damage. Works synergistically with Vitamin C. Also functions as a mild emollient and skin conditioner.
AntioxidantAM + PM
Green Tea Extract
Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract
Rich in polyphenols (EGCG), green tea extract may provide antioxidant support and has a skin-soothing profile. Used in formulas where environmental resilience and calm are combined.
AntioxidantSoothing
Resveratrol
Resveratrol (plant-derived)
A polyphenol antioxidant found in certain plants. Research suggests it may support the skin's antioxidant defences against environmental stressors. Used in targeted treatment formulations.
AntioxidantPM Use
Gentle Renewal

Exfoliating Ingredients

Used at appropriate concentrations to support skin renewal without compromising barrier integrity.

Lactic Acid
Lactic Acid (AHA)
A gentle alpha-hydroxy acid (AHA) derived from fermentation. At concentrations of 5–10%, it may support surface-level exfoliation and skin texture improvement. Well-suited to beginners and sensitive skin types compared to stronger AHAs.
AHAGentlePM Use
Salicylic Acid
Salicylic Acid (BHA)
A beta-hydroxy acid (BHA) that is oil-soluble, allowing it to penetrate into pores. At 1–2%, it may support clearer-looking skin and is commonly used in formulas for oily and breakout-prone skin types and targeted evening routines.
BHAOily SkinPM Use
Mandelic Acid
Mandelic Acid (AHA)
A larger-molecule AHA derived from bitter almonds. Its slower absorption rate makes it suitable for sensitive skin types seeking the benefits of chemical exfoliation with a lower irritation profile during regular use and ongoing care over time.
AHASensitivePM Use
Our Exclusion List

Ingredients We Choose Not to Use

Luméra maintains a clear list of ingredients we exclude — based on current evidence, known sensitisation risk, or lack of demonstrated benefit. This list is reviewed regularly.

Potential endocrine disruption concerns
Synthetic Fragrances
Common sensitiser — especially for reactive skin
SLS / SLES
Potential irritant in repeated-use cleansers
Formaldehyde Releasers
Known sensitisers — DMDM Hydantoin, IPBC
Mineral Oil (heavy)
Occlusive without added skin benefit
Artificial Dyes
No benefit — potential sensitiser
Oxybenzone (chemical SPF)
Environmental and potential systemic concerns
Undisclosed Blends
