Ingredient Spotlight

Hyaluronic Acid

A naturally occurring molecule found in the skin that may help bind and retain water. A single gram can hold up to six litres of water — making it one of the most studied hydration ingredients in skincare formulation.

Luméra formulations use a multi-weight hyaluronic acid blend to support hydration at multiple skin depths. Suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin.
