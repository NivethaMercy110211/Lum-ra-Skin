Our Story

"We started Luméra because we believed the skincare industry owed its customers more clarity."

Our Founding

Built on the Belief That Skincare Should Be Honest

Luméra Skin was founded in 2021 by a team of formulation chemists and clinical skincare practitioners who were tired of vague ingredient labels, unsupported claims and confusing product choices.

We believed then — and still believe — that customers deserve to know exactly what they're applying to their skin. Not just the ingredient name, but the function it serves, the concentration it's included at, and the evidence behind it.

The name "Luméra" draws from the Latin lumen — meaning light. We use it as a metaphor for clarity: in our formulations, in our communication, and in our relationship with our customers.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles shape every formulation decision, every ingredient choice and every customer interaction at Luméra Skin.

Radical Transparency
Every ingredient disclosed. Every function explained. No proprietary blends or hidden actives.
Evidence-Informed

Our Journey

From Concept to Collection

2021
Luméra Skin Founded
Started by a team of formulators, dermatologists and ingredient scientists with a shared belief in transparency.
2022
Core Serum Range Launched
Our Hyaluronic Acid, Niacinamide and Vitamin C serums became the foundation of the range — all receiving independent dermatological review.
2023
Ingredient Transparency Platform Launched
We became one of the first skincare brands to publish full INCI names, concentrations and functions for every formulation.
2024
Skin Type Guide and Quiz Introduced
Launched the skin type quiz and personalised guide — helping customers match products to their actual skin behaviour rather than categories.
2026
36-Product Range + Bundle Collection
Our most complete range yet — now with curated bundles, an expanded eye care line and our first mineral SPF offerings.
